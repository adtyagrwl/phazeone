Parking

Free customer parking at the Marlowe Avenue shops car park — directly outside the salon. It's a small car park shared with the other shops on the parade, so it can fill up at peak times, but you can usually find a space within a minute's walk.

On-street parking on Marlowe Avenue itself is limited and restricted. If the shops car park is full, Greenbridge Retail Park is about a 5-minute walk away.

By bus: the Marlowe Avenue Shops stop is a 1-minute walk. Routes 2 and 5.

Before you book

Is Phaze One unisex?

Yes. Phaze One is a full-service unisex salon serving both men and women. Haircuts, colour, and beauty treatments are available for all.

Do you do acrylic nails and nail art?

Yes. Acrylic nails are a core service, and 3D acrylic nail art is available on request. The nail technician has over 11 years of industry experience.

Is there parking at the salon?

Yes — free customer parking is available at the Marlowe Avenue shops car park directly outside the salon. It's a small car park shared with other shops, so it can be busy at peak times. The nearest bus stop is Marlowe Avenue Shops (1 minute walk, routes 2 and 5).

How do I book an appointment?

Call 01793 526985. Walk-ins are sometimes possible, but booking ahead is recommended — especially for colour, balayage, and nail art.

Where is Phaze One in Swindon?

67 Marlowe Avenue, Walcot East, Swindon SN3 3HQ. It's on the parade of shops, a 1-minute walk from the Marlowe Avenue Shops bus stop.

Do you take card payments?

Call ahead to confirm — the salon accepts card and cash, but it's worth checking before a large booking.
